## The classic Frogger game flipped!  
FROGS HATE YOU! takes the classic retro arcade game of Frogger and flips the roles. YOU hate frogs and they hate you as they try to cross the street of peril to eat your delicious flies. Plan out intricate defenses and even take to the ground yourself to defend against these amphibian abominations!  

## Controls
### Tower View  
**Esc** - Pause Menu  
**F** - Switch Perspective  
**Left Mouse** - Select and place tower  
**W** - Move Forward  
**A** - Move Left  
**S** - Move Back  
**D** - Move Right 
### Player View  
**Esc** - Pause Menu
**F** - Switch Perspective  
**W** - Move Forward  
**A** - Move Left  
**S** - Move Back  
**D** - Move Right 
**Left Mouse** - Kick  
**Right Mouse** - Suction Gun  
**E** - Interact
